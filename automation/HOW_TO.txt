NBA SAVANT UPDATES (manual only)

There is no scheduled update anymore. Nothing runs unless you start it.

To update the site:
  - Ask Claude in chat to update it, or
  - Double-click "Update NBA Savant.command" in the nba-savant folder yourself.

Each update: pulls new stats from NBA.com, rebuilds index.html, pushes it to GitHub,
and Cloudflare publishes it about a minute later. Your Mac must be on.
Log: ~/Library/Logs/nba-savant-update.log
